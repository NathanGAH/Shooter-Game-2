
---- CONTROLS ----
WASD or Arrow keys to move
Space to shoot
H for shield (cool down 30s)
M for boomerang (cool down 5s)
L for bouncy sawblade (cool down 30s)
P for auto-targeting bullet (cool down 0s; can only be shot 1 time)
enter for kaboom (cool down 15s)

---- ENEMY TYPES ----
Regular Enemy; follows you, deals 1 damage, dies in one hit.

Boss Enemy; follows you, deals 999999999 damage (crashes your game), dies in some amount of hits.

Regular Friend; follows you, gives 1 health, dies in one hit.

Super Friend; follows you, gives max health, does not die except from kabooms.

(+); circles around you (enter), gives 1 health on touching you or on shot, dies in one hit.

(-); circles around you (exit), deals 1 damage on touching you or on shot, dies in one hit.

EIS (Everyone In Shock); just bounces around you, doesnt do anything good or bad.

---- BULLET TYPES ----
Regular Bullet; travels in one direction, dies when it touches an enemy or the edge.

Shield; follows the player, still deals damage like a bullet. (Ticks alive: ~650)

Boomerang; launches in the direction of the player, and comes back after touching the edge.

Bouncy Sawblade; bounces, is a bullet. (Ticks alive: 600)

Auto-Targeting Bullet; targets a random enemy until it dies, then chooses another random enemy to target. (Ticks alive: Infinity)
